FAQ and support

What does the model scale mean?

Scale shows how the model compares to the full-size vehicle. At 1:64, a measurement on the model is one sixty-fourth of the original. Check the product page for listed dimensions.

Can I pay with Cash on Delivery?

Enter your pincode on a product page to check COD availability. Checkout shows any COD and delivery fees before you place your order.

Where can I track or cancel an order?

Open the tracking link on the confirmation page, or find it in your order history. If you checked out as a guest, use the same browser. Orders can be cancelled while placed or confirmed.

Where can I learn about shipping and returns?

See the shipping policy and returns policy for store information.

How do I get a restock alert?

Select “Notify me when back in stock” on a sold-out model. You’ll see a notice in the store when you return in the same browser after it has restocked.
